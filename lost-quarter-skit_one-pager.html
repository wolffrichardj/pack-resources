Lost Quarter

Cub Scout skit • indoor • simple props • about 1 minute

This is a very easy skit for younger Scouts because the action repeats, the joke is clear, and the prop list is minimal. It works especially well for Lions and Tigers with a little leader prompting.

Cast 4–8 Scouts
Props Optional flashlight
Length About 1 minute
Best for Lions and Tigers

Setup

  • One Scout stands still like a lamppost and points a flashlight at the ground, if using one.
  • Scout 1 is on the floor searching under the light.
  • Other Scouts enter one at a time and ask what is happening.
  • The humor comes from repeating the same action, then landing the final punchline clearly.

Script

Stage note
One Scout stands still as a lamppost. If you have a flashlight, point it at the ground. Scout 1 is searching where the light is shining.
Scout 2
Hey, what are you looking for?
Scout 1
I lost a quarter.
Scout 2
I will help you find it.
Stage note
Scout 2 starts searching in the same spot. Additional Scouts come in one at a time and repeat the same pattern.
Scout 3
Hey, what are you looking for?
Scout 1
I lost a quarter.
Scout 3
I will help you find it.
Stage note
Repeat with as many Scouts as you want.
Scout 2
I do not see it. Are you sure you lost it here?
Scout 1
No. I lost it over there.
Scout 2
Then why are we looking over here?
Scout 1
Because the light is better over here!
Leader note

For Lions, let the lamppost role be non-speaking and keep only 3–4 speaking parts. A leader can quietly cue the repeated lines from the front row.
