The Speaker's Guide

Tiger

Narrator: Tigers, you have spent this year prowling through new experiences, earning your stripes by working together and serving your community. You have shown that a Tiger is helpful, friendly, and courteous.

As you prepare to move deeper into the forest as Wolves, we mark your journey.

Assistant: Apply the Orange Flames/Stripes to the cheeks.

First, we place orange stripes upon your cheeks. These represent the fire of your enthusiasm and the energy you bring to our Pack. They show you are a scout of action!

Assistant: Apply the Brown Trail/Line across the forehead.

Next, we draw a brown line across your forehead. This represents the earth and the trails you have walked together. It reminds you to stay grounded and focused on the path ahead.

Assistant: Apply the Blue Dot to the chin.

Finally, we place a blue dot upon your chin. This is the mark of the Pack. It anchors you to our family, reminding you that you never walk the trail alone.

Tigers, the pack is proud of you. Cross now into your new den!
