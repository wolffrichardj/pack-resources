The Speaker's Guide

Bear

Narrator: Bears, you have shown the strength and determination that your rank implies. You have tackled new challenges with courage and learned the skills of the outdoors. You have truly stood tall this year.

As you prepare to begin your journey as Webelos, we honor your achievements.

Assistant: Apply the Light Blue Arcs under the eyes.

First, we place light blue arcs under your eyes. These represent the wide horizons you have explored and the clear vision you have developed. You see the world with a Scout's eyes now.

Assistant: Apply the White Dot to the forehead.

Next, we place a white dot upon your forehead. This is the mark of wisdom and the knowledge you have gained. It reminds you to always use your head and your heart together.

Assistant: Apply the Blue Foundation Bars to the cheeks.

Finally, we place blue bars upon your cheeks. These represent the solid foundation of character you have built. They show you are strong, steady, and ready for the trail ahead.

Bears, your new path awaits. Cross over and join the Webelos den!
