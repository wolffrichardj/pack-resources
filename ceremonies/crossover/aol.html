The Speaker's Guide

AOL

Narrator: Will our Arrow of Light candidates please step forward. Tonight, we honor the pinnacle of the Cub Scouting journey. These Scouts have climbed the entire mountain, proven their leadership, and are now prepared to leave the safety of the Pack to embark on the greater adventures of a Scouts BSA Troop.

Assistant: Apply the Gold Rays to the forehead.

I paint you with Gold, representing the glowing sun on your emblem, its seven rays for each day of the week, and the bright light of leadership you carry forward.

Assistant: Apply the Green Bars to the cheeks.

I paint you with Green, for your advanced outdoor mastery, your commitment to the wilderness, and the deep forests you will soon explore.

Assistant: Apply the White Vertical line to the nose.

I paint you with White, symbolizing the highest tier of honor, integrity, and your promise to live out the Scout Oath and Law in your daily life.

Assistant: Apply the Blue Circle to the chin.

I paint you with Blue, completing the circle of your Cub Scout trail, honoring your years of loyalty, and forever marking you as a permanent part of Pack 285.

Arrow of Light Scouts, your trail with us is complete, and your arrows point straight toward the horizon. We proudly present you with the ultimate Cub Scout honor: your Arrow of Light awards!
