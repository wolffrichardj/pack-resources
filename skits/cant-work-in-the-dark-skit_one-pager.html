Can't Work in the Dark

Cub Scout skit · simple props · About 1 minute

A work crew has an unusual helper. When that helper leaves, the whole crew takes a break.

Cast5-8 Scouts
PropsNone
LengthAbout 1 minute
Best forAny den

Setup

  • One Scout is the supervisor, one is the lightbulb, and 3-6 are workers.
  • Workers mime raking leaves. The lightbulb stands nearby with one hand raised.

Script

Supervisor
Everyone is busy except you. What is your job?
Lightbulb
I'm the lightbulb!
Supervisor
We need help raking. Please put your hand down and join us.
Stage note
The supervisor walks away. Workers keep raking; the lightbulb keeps a hand raised.
Supervisor
Still standing there? You can take your break now.
Lightbulb
Okay!
Stage note
The lightbulb lowers their hand and exits. All the workers immediately stop raking.
Supervisor
Why did everybody stop? There are still leaves to rake!
Worker 1
We can't work in the dark!
Stage note
The workers shrug and exit together. The supervisor looks up at the missing "light," shakes their head, and exits.
Leader note

The lightbulb leaving is the cue for every worker to stop together. Keep the supervisor friendly and puzzled. Rehearse the final line slowly and clearly; use imaginary tools and normal room lighting.
