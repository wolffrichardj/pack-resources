A Bright Idea

Cub Scout STEM skit • indoor • simple props • 1–2 minutes

This skit is a simple way to connect STEM night to inventing, creativity, and problem-solving. The joke is easy for younger Scouts to understand, and the setup is light enough to run with almost no prep.

Cast 4–6 Scouts
Props Chair, box, toy airplane, clothes, string
Length About 1–2 minutes
Best for Pack STEM meeting

Setup

  • One Scout sits in a chair, thinking hard.
  • Scatter a few safe props around to suggest a messy room.
  • The other Scouts walk in together.
  • Keep the lines upbeat and moving.

Script

Stage note
Scout 1 is sitting in a chair. Toys and clothes are scattered around the room. Scout 1 looks deep in thought. The other Scouts enter.
All Scouts
Hi. What are you doing?
Scout 1
I am working on a bright idea.
Scout 2
What kind of idea?
Scout 1
An invention.
Scout 3
A real invention?
Scout 1
Yes. I want to invent something useful.
Scout 4
Can we help?
Scout 1
Definitely. Bring me that box. I also need the toy airplane, those clothes, and that string.
Stage note
The other Scouts gather the items and place them in the box.
Scout 2
Okay, everything is in the box. Now what?
Scout 1
That should do it.
Scout 3
Do what?
Scout 1
I just invented the fastest way to clean my room before my parent gets home.
All Scouts
That is a bright idea!
Leader note

After the skit, you can ask the audience what problem the Scout was trying to solve and whether the solution counts as an invention.
