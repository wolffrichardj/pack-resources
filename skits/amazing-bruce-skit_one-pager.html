The Amazing Bruce

Cub Scout skit · simple props · 1 minute + callback

A team launches an imaginary acrobat high into the sky. His return becomes a surprise later in the program.

Cast5-7 Scouts
PropsNone
Length1 minute + callback
Best forPack campfire

Setup

  • One announcer and 4-6 team members form a semicircle, holding an imaginary blanket.
  • Bruce is imaginary. Agree with the program leader when the team will return.

Script

Announcer
Presenting the Amazing Bruce and our world-famous blanket team!
Stage note
The team looks at the imaginary Bruce in the blanket, then gently "tosses" him and follows him with their eyes.
Announcer
A little higher! One, two, three!
Stage note
Toss again. Everyone looks higher, then tracks Bruce back into the blanket.
Announcer
Bruce wants to try for a record! Give him a cheer!
Announcer
One, two, THREE!
Stage note
Everyone tosses and looks far overhead. Pause, still looking up.
Announcer
That may take a while. We'll be back!
Stage note
Everyone exits. At the agreed cue after another skit or song, the announcer returns.
Announcer
Here comes Bruce! Team, get ready!
Stage note
The team returns, forms the blanket, watches Bruce descend, and gently "catches" him. Everyone bows toward Bruce and cheers. Exit.
Leader note

Rehearse looking and tossing together; Bruce should be in the same place for everyone. Never lift or throw a person. Arrange the callback in advance and keep the team nearby so the ending is not forgotten.

Printable handout for any pack or den. Adapted from a traditional skit; wording and staging revised.
