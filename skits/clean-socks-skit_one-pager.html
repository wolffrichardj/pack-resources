Clean Socks

Cub Scout skit · simple props · 1-2 minutes

Each Scout seems cleaner than the last. Then twelve pairs turns out to mean one pair per month.

Cast5 Scouts
PropsClean socks optional
Length1-2 minutes
Best forWolves and older

Setup

  • One helper faces the audience. Scouts 1-4 queue beside the helper in number order.
  • Each Scout steps forward, answers, takes a pretend bundle of socks, then stands aside. Keep the last answer a surprise.

Script

Helper
Fresh socks! How many pairs do you need?
Scout 1
Two. I wear one pair all week while the other is in the wash.
Helper
A whole week? Phew! Here you go. Next!
Scout 2
Four pairs. A fresh pair on Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Sunday.
Helper
That's better! Here you go. Next!
Scout 3
Seven pairs, please.
Helper
One clean pair every day?
Scout 3
Exactly!
Helper
Now that's clean! Here you go. Next!
Scout 4
Twelve pairs, please!
Helper
Twelve? Even cleaner! Why twelve?
Scout 4
One for January, one for February, one for March...
Stage note
Pause. The helper looks shocked; the other Scouts react in disbelief. Hold for laughter, then everyone exits.
Leader note

Rehearse each turn: step forward, answer, collect socks, step aside. The helper reacts with disgust, relief, approval, admiration, then shock. Say the months proudly and slowly; stop at March. Explain privately that monthly changes are the joke: change socks daily and when wet or dirty.
