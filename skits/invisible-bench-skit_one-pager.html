Invisible Bench

Cub Scout skit · simple props · About 1 minute

Scouts settle onto an imaginary bench. When the last Scout says it was moved, everyone suddenly loses their seat.

Cast4-8 Scouts
PropsNone
LengthAbout 1 minute
Best forAny den

Setup

  • Scout 1 starts in a shallow squat, facing the audience as if seated. Reserve the last Scout as the bench mover; that Scout never joins the seat.
  • Scouts 2 through the next-to-last enter one at a time and sit beside Scout 1. Leave enough space for the rehearsed pretend fall.

Script

Scout 2
What are you doing?
Scout 1
Sitting on this invisible bench.
Scout 2
Can I sit with you?
Scout 1
Sure!
Stage note
Scout 2 joins the pretend seat. Each remaining sitter repeats Scout 2's questions; Scout 1 repeats the answers. The bench mover enters last and stays standing.
Bench mover
What are you all doing?
All seated
Sitting on the invisible bench!
Stage note
The mover points to a different spot.
Bench mover
But I moved it over there this morning!
Stage note
On "morning," all sitters cry "Whoa!" and pretend the seat disappears: lower carefully into a seated position on the ground. The mover stays standing. Hold for laughter, then get up and exit.
Leader note

Rehearse the ending first on clear, level ground: a controlled sit-down, never a backward drop. Use shallow squats and quick entrances. Where sitting down is unsuitable, dip lower and throw hands forward as if losing the seat. The reaction goes DOWN, not up; the missing support is the punchline.
