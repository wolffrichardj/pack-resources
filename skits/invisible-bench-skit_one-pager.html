Invisible Bench

Cub Scout skit · simple props · About 1 minute

An imaginary seat fills up with Scouts, until someone reveals that the bench has moved.

Cast4-8 Scouts
PropsNone
LengthAbout 1 minute
Best forAny den

Setup

  • Scout 1 stands with knees slightly bent, pretending to sit. Other Scouts enter one at a time.
  • Choose the final Scout before starting. Leave space for everyone to straighten up safely.

Script

Scout 2
What are you doing?
Scout 1
Sitting on this invisible bench.
Scout 2
Can I join you?
Scout 1
Sure! There is plenty of room.
Stage note
Scout 2 pretends to sit beside Scout 1. Each additional Scout asks the same questions and joins the row.
Final Scout
What are you all doing?
All seated
Sitting on this invisible bench!
Final Scout
But I moved that bench over there this morning!
Stage note
Everyone looks down, straightens up in surprise, and says "Whoa!" together. Exit.
Leader note

Practice the imaginary seat and the final reaction once before adding lines. Use shallow knee bends or stand upright with hands resting on an imaginary seat; nobody falls. Keep each entrance quick.
