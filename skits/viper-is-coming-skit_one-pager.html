The Viper Is Coming

Cub Scout skit · simple props · About 1 minute

Scouts expect a scary visitor. A cheerful window cleaner arrives, and the audience hears what "viper" really means.

Cast5 Scouts
PropsCleaning cloth
LengthAbout 1 minute
Best forWolves and older

Setup

  • Assign Scouts 1-4 as messengers and Scout 5 as the window wiper. Only the wiper uses W-to-V pronunciation.
  • Scout 1 enters first. Each messenger gives a warning, then stays in a nervous huddle facing the entrance. The wiper waits out of sight with a cloth.

Script

Scout 1
The Viper is coming!
Scout 2
I heard the Viper is heading this way!
Scout 3
Look out! The Viper is almost here!
Scout 4
Here comes the Viper!
Stage note
The messengers huddle back. The wiper walks in calmly, holding the cloth, and smiles at them.
Wiper
Hello! I'm the viper. I've come to vipe your vindows!
Stage note
The wiper starts polishing an imaginary window. The messengers look at the cloth, then at one another as they understand. Hold for laughter, then everyone exits.
Leader note

Teach the reveal first: "wiper, wipe, windows" becomes "viper, vipe, vindows." Use an exaggerated W-to-V accent only for the cleaner; say the line slowly and cheerfully. No sign is needed. Rehearse entrances in order, keep warnings brief, and let the last word land before the reactions.
