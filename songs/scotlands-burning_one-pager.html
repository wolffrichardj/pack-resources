Scotland's Burning

1915 round · Four phrases

Sing all four lines together first. Then sing as a round.

Lyrics

Scotland's burning, Scotland's burning,
Look out, look out!
Fire, fire, fire, fire!
Pour on water, Pour on water.

Leader note

Divide into two to four groups. Group 1 begins. Group 2 starts at 'Scotland's burning' when Group 1 reaches 'Look out'; Group 3 when Group 1 reaches 'Fire'; Group 4 when Group 1 reaches 'Pour on water.' Repeat the four lines. On the leader's last-pass cue, each group finishes its own four lines.
