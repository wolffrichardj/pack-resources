John Jacob Jingleheimer Schmidt

Traditional camp song · Quiet-to-loud game

Repeat the verse, getting quieter each time. Always sing the final "Fa-la-la-la-la-la-la!" line loudly.

Verse

John Jacob Jingleheimer Schmidt.
His name is my name too!
Whenever we go out,
the people always shout.
They say John Jacob Jingleheimer Schmidt,
Fa-la-la-la-la-la-la!
