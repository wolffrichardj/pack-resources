Crossover Ceremony

Pack 285 Crossover Ceremony Guide

Overview

The Crossover Ceremony centers on celebrating the hard work, memories, and specific achievements the Scouts completed during the past year. It is a transition that honors the past while looking forward to the future.

How it Works (5 Steps)

  1. The Call Forward: The Scouts of a specific den are called to the front of the room to stand before the Pack.
    Assistant: Have paint and brushes ready for the specific rank.
  2. The Narrative: The Cubmaster or Den Leader reads the rank-specific script. This story ties their achievements to the symbolic marks they are about to receive.
  3. The Symbolic Painting: As the narrative is read (or immediately after), an assistant applies simple paint marks to the Scout's face. Each mark represents a "Milestone" reached.
  4. The Crossover: Once painted, the Scouts cross the bridge or symbolic threshold to meet their new Den Leader or Scoutmaster.
  5. The Welcome: The new leaders welcome them with their new rank neckerchief, slide, or a simple handshake, officially marking their transition.
